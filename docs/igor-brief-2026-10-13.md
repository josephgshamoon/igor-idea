## The document: 24 feature areas

**1. AI Personal Assistant**

One global AI assistant for travel, everyday tasks, planning,
recommendations, voice commands, personalised help and taking actions
with user permission.

**2. Global Travel Guide and AI Trip Planner**

Discover destinations worldwide, plan trips by dates and budget, create
daily itineraries, compare options and receive recommendations based on
interests and preferences.

**3. Global Public Transport, Taxi and Route Planner**

Metro, buses, trams, trains, ferries, taxis, ride-hailing, car rentals,
intercity travel, inter-emirate routes, international connections, maps,
stations, stops, timetables, fares, tickets, step-by-step directions,
journey times, transport comparisons, live traffic, delays and
accessibility. Include routes between Dubai, Abu Dhabi and all other
emirates, plus new rail connections such as the planned Etihad Rail
passenger network where services and schedules are officially available.

**4. Flights, Airports and International Travel**

Flight search and booking, fare comparisons, baggage rules, connections,
airport navigation, check-in instructions, flight status, terminals,
boarding gates, departure reminders and travel disruption alerts.

**5. Hotels and Accommodation**

Search, compare, book and manage hotels, apartments, hostels and other
accommodation worldwide, including prices, amenities, locations,
availability and cancellation conditions.

**6. Restaurants, Food and Reservations**

Discover restaurants and cafés, compare menus and prices, check opening
hours, find suitable dietary options, book tables, manage reservations
and request booking changes.

**7. Attractions, Tours, Activities and Events**

Discover and book tourist attractions, guided tours, museums, theme
parks, beaches, concerts, exhibitions, festivals, sporting events and
entertainment.

**8. Global Maps and Local Navigation**

Interactive maps, walking and driving directions, nearby places, saved
locations, route alternatives, estimated arrival times and navigation
between transport stations and destinations.

**9. Real-Time AI Translation and Communication**

Translate text, speech, live conversations, messages, emails, signs,
menus and documents; support camera translation, voice interaction and
multilingual communication.

**10. Local Culture, Laws, Visas and Travel Requirements**

Country-specific customs, etiquette, local regulations, visa and entry
requirements, passport validity guidance, transit rules, official
information sources and links to relevant authorities.

**11. Weather, Currency and Local Information**

Weather forecasts, severe-weather alerts, currency conversion, local
time, opening hours, public holidays and other location-specific
information.

**12. Shopping, Deals and Local Services**

Discover shops, malls, supermarkets, pharmacies, repair services,
salons, laundry services, local offers and other businesses, with
available prices, locations and booking options.

**13. Emergency, Safety and Healthcare Assistance**

Verified local emergency numbers, police, ambulance, fire services,
hospitals, pharmacies, embassy contacts, safety guidance, accessibility
support and emergency instructions relevant to the user's location.

**14. Digital Travel Itinerary and Document Storage**

Organise trips, bookings, tickets, boarding passes, reservations, travel
documents, addresses and daily schedules in one secure place.

**15. Booking Management and AI Actions**

Manage supported flight, hotel, restaurant, transport and activity
bookings; request changes and cancellations; contact providers; compare
alternatives; and obtain approval before completing consequential
actions.

**16. AI Email, Messaging and Provider Communication**

Draft and translate emails, prepare messages, contact hotels or
restaurants, communicate with service providers and follow up on
requests through supported integrations, with permission.

**17. Smart Reminders, Calendar and Proactive Assistance**

Manage appointments, tasks and schedules; remind users about check-in,
boarding, reservations and departures; and provide timely alerts based
on traffic, weather, delays and itinerary changes.

**18. Personalised Recommendations and Daily Planning**

Recommend destinations, restaurants, activities, routes and services
based on user-approved preferences, available time, budget, location and
plans.

**19. Budget Planner, Expenses, Payments and Rewards**

Estimate trip costs, track expenses, compare currencies and prices,
support available payment providers, manage approved payments and
organise loyalty programmes and rewards.

**20. Family, Group and Business Travel**

Coordinate group itineraries, shared bookings, family preferences,
business trips, meeting schedules, travel expenses and accessibility
requirements.

**21. User Profile, Memory and Personal Preferences**

Manage language, travel style, favourite places, budget, dietary
requirements, accessibility needs and other user-approved preferences.

**22. Multilingual App and Voice Interface**

Provide app navigation, AI responses, voice commands and customer
assistance in supported languages, with local language expansion over
time.

**23. Privacy, Security and User Permissions**

Secure accounts and documents, control location and data access, require
approval for purchases and sensitive actions, provide transaction
histories and allow users to manage or delete personal data.

**24. Global Integrations and Country Expansion**

Connect reliable maps, transport operators, rail networks, booking
providers, payment systems, calendars, messaging services and official
information sources. Expand country by country with local languages,
currencies, laws, transport data and service availability.
